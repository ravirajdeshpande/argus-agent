# Supply-Chain Resilience Strategy — FY2026

**Demo data notice:** Fictional executive strategy.

## Strategic objectives
1. Reduce single-source dependency for critical components.
2. Improve visibility into supplier sites and sub-tier dependencies.
3. Shorten time from external event detection to business-impact assessment.
4. Preserve compliance and product-quality gates during sourcing changes.

## Priorities
- Identify critical components with less than six weeks of usable inventory.
- Maintain documented qualification status for alternate suppliers and manufacturing sites.
- Require evidence-backed risk summaries with explicit confidence and unresolved questions.
- Review high-impact supply risks monthly and after material regulatory events.

## Decision rights
The agent may gather evidence, score provisional risk, and recommend actions. Procurement, Engineering, Compliance, Legal, and executive owners retain their respective approval authority.
