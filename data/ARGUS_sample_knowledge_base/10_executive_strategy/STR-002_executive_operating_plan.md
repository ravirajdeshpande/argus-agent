# Q4 Operating Plan — Critical Component Readiness

**Demo data notice:** Fictional operating plan.

## Outcomes
- Validate one alternate source for each designated single-source critical component where technically feasible.
- Improve supplier delivery confirmation and inventory freshness.
- Establish a weekly review for components below four weeks of coverage.
- Ensure every material risk report links claims to source records.

## Measures
| Measure | Target | Review cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Critical supplier site records reviewed | 100% | Monthly |
| Material risk claims with evidence references | 100% | Per report |
| Components below four weeks of coverage with an assigned owner | 100% | Weekly |
| High-impact recommendations with recorded approval | 100% | Per decision |

## Constraints
No alternate supplier is considered production-ready until required engineering, quality, security, and compliance approvals are complete. Targets are demo operating objectives, not externally validated benchmarks.
