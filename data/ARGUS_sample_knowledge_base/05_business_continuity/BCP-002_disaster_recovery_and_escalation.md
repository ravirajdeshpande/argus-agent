# Operational Disruption Escalation and Recovery

**Demo data notice:** Fictional policy for testing workflows.

## Severity levels
- **Level 1:** Local issue with no expected customer impact and recovery within 2 business days.
- **Level 2:** Potential production impact or recovery expected to exceed 2 business days.
- **Level 3:** Critical product interruption, regulatory prohibition, or multiple-site disruption.

## Escalation
- Level 1: Operations Manager.
- Level 2: Operations Manager and Supply Chain Risk Lead.
- Level 3: Executive Incident Lead, Legal/Compliance, Procurement, and Product Operations.

## Recovery planning
Each incident record must include a timeline, impacted products, inventory assumptions, workaround status, next update time, and decision owner. If evidence is incomplete, mark the assessment provisional.

## ARGUS guardrail
ARGUS may prepare a recovery recommendation but must not issue supplier instructions, change purchase orders, or commit spending without authorized human approval.
