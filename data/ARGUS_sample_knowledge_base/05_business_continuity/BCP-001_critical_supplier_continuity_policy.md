# Critical Supplier Continuity Policy

**Demo data notice:** Fictional internal policy for the ARGUS demo.

## Purpose
Maintain continuity of critical products when a supplier, site, transport route, or region experiences disruption.

## Trigger thresholds
- A forecast interruption exceeding 10 business days triggers a supply-planning review.
- Projected coverage below 4 weeks for a critical component triggers escalation to the Supply Chain Risk Lead.
- A confirmed regulatory restriction affecting a critical component triggers Legal and Compliance review.

## Required response
1. Confirm the affected component, supplier, site, and product.
2. Validate usable inventory and firm inbound receipts.
3. Request a written recovery plan from the supplier.
4. Assess approved alternatives and qualification lead time.
5. Record decisions, owners, deadlines, and unresolved assumptions.

## Approval
Changes to an approved source, manufacturing site, or product specification require documented quality and compliance approval.
