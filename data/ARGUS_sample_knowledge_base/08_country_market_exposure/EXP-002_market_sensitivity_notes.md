# Market Sensitivity Notes — Electronics Supply Chain

**Demo data notice:** Fictional analytical note, not investment advice.

## Sensitivity factors
- Export-control changes may affect availability only when the covered item, transaction, parties, and jurisdiction meet the rule's scope.
- Freight disruption may increase lead time even when the item remains legally exportable.
- Currency movement can change landed cost where supplier invoices and company budgets use different currencies.
- Capacity constraints may amplify a delay when a component has a single approved source.

## Evidence needed before impact scoring
Current official notices, supplier statements, open purchase orders, inventory coverage, component classification, relevant market data, and documented alternatives.

## Reporting rule
Separate confirmed facts from scenario assumptions. Avoid assigning a causal link between a market movement and a regulatory event unless the evidence supports it.
