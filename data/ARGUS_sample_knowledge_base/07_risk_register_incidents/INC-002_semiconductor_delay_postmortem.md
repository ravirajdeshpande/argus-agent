# Incident Review — Semiconductor Shipment Delay

**Demo data notice:** Fictional historical incident.

## Summary
A shipment of NS-440 modules arrived 11 calendar days later than the supplier's original estimate after a port congestion event and incomplete shipping documentation.

## Impact
- One Orion production batch was rescheduled.
- No customer delivery was missed.
- The reported impact was mitigated by reallocating 2,000 usable units from another program after approval.

## Root causes and contributing factors
- Logistics disruption.
- Shipping documents were received later than planned.
- The purchase order had no confirmed alternate shipment route.

## Lessons learned
- Confirm logistics milestones for critical shipments.
- Distinguish supplier-confirmed dates from internal estimates.
- Escalate when projected component coverage approaches four weeks.
- Document approval for cross-program inventory reallocation.

## Evidence limits
This incident does not establish that a future delay will have the same cause or impact.
