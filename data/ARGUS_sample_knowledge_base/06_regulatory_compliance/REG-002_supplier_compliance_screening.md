# Supplier Compliance Screening Procedure

**Demo data notice:** Fictional internal procedure.

## Required checks
- Confirm the legal entity name and relevant aliases.
- Confirm manufacturing site and country of origin for the item under review.
- Check current official restricted-party and applicable regulatory sources.
- Confirm whether a license, permit, or documented exception may be required.
- Record screening timestamp, source, reviewer, disposition, and any follow-up.

## Status meanings
- **Clear:** No issue identified in the sources checked at the recorded time.
- **Review required:** Identity, scope, or rule applicability is uncertain.
- **Hold:** Do not proceed until authorized compliance clearance is documented.

## Freshness
A prior screening result is not a permanent clearance. Re-screen when a relevant event, transaction, supplier change, or rule update occurs.
