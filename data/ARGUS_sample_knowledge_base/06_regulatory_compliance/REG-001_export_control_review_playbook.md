# Export-Control Review Playbook

**Demo data notice:** Fictional internal guidance; not legal advice and not a statement of actual law.

## Review sequence
1. Locate the official government notice or regulation text.
2. Identify jurisdiction, publication date, effective date, covered items, technical thresholds, destination, end user, and transaction scope.
3. Compare the actual component specifications and classification with the rule.
4. Check applicable parties, licenses, exceptions, and transition provisions.
5. Record the source URL or document ID and the exact passage supporting each material conclusion.
6. Refer ambiguous or high-impact interpretations to Trade Compliance and Legal.

## Evidence standard
News reports may identify a potential issue but should not alone establish the legal scope of a restriction. If no primary source is available, preserve the uncertainty and lower the confidence assessment.

## Prohibited inference
A country or company mention in a news story does not, by itself, prove that a specific shipment is prohibited.
