# Supplier Agreement — Northstar Semiconductor Ltd.

**Demo data notice:** This fictional agreement is for the ARGUS proof of concept and is not legal advice.

## Commercial scope
Northstar Semiconductor Ltd. supplies NS-440 power-management modules and NS-880 embedded controllers used in the Orion Edge Gateway and Atlas Vision Controller.

## Delivery and continuity terms
- Standard lead time: 8 weeks after accepted purchase order.
- Supplier must notify the buyer within 5 business days of a material production interruption.
- For a force-majeure event expected to delay shipments by more than 14 calendar days, the supplier must provide a recovery plan and revised delivery schedule.
- Buyer may request a quarterly capacity and continuity review.

## Change and compliance obligations
Supplier must notify the buyer of material changes to manufacturing location, product specification, export classification, or ownership. Shipments remain subject to applicable export controls and sanctions.
