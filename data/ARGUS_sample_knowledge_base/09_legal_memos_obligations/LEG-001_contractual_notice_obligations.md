# Legal Review Note — Supplier Disruption Notices

**Demo data notice:** Fictional draft for testing; not legal advice.

## Question
What should the business check when a critical supplier reports a likely delivery interruption?

## Review checklist
- Identify the signed agreement and current amendments.
- Confirm the notice period and triggering event in the actual contract.
- Check force-majeure, allocation, cure, termination, and mitigation clauses.
- Confirm governing law and notice delivery method.
- Preserve supplier communications and internal decisions.
- Escalate disputed interpretations to counsel.

## ARGUS handling
This note is a checklist, not a determination of rights under any particular contract. ARGUS must retrieve the controlling signed agreement and amendments before summarizing specific obligations.
