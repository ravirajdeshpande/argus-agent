# Legal Triage Checklist — New Regulatory Event

**Demo data notice:** Fictional internal checklist; not legal advice.

## Intake
Record the official notice, issuing authority, jurisdiction, publication date, effective date, covered goods/technology, parties, destinations, and transition provisions.

## Exposure mapping
Link the rule to the component classification, supplier entity, manufacturing site, shipping route, end user, and open transaction. Mark any missing attribute explicitly.

## Decision categories
- **No exposure identified in reviewed scope:** evidence supports no current match, subject to stated limitations.
- **Potential exposure:** one or more required attributes remain unresolved.
- **Likely exposure requiring review:** evidence indicates a plausible match; authorized counsel/compliance must determine the action.
- **Confirmed restriction:** use only when an authorized reviewer records the basis and disposition.

## External actions
No shipment hold, supplier notice, purchase-order cancellation, or legal position may be executed solely on an autonomous agent recommendation.
